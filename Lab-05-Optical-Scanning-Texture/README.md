# Lab 05: Optical Scanning - Ataturk Model (Color & Texture)

## Project Objective
The mission objective is to execute a high-resolution color scan of the Ataturk model utilizing a rotary table, focusing on optimal hardware alignment, HDR settings, and RGB texture mapping.

## Engineering Constraints & Criteria
- **Hardware Calibration**: The rotary table must be calibrated for 36 steps (10° increments).
- **Scanning Parameters**: HDR and Texture Scan features must be enabled for optimal color and geometry capture.
- **Validation Report**: Must deliver a PDF containing a scanning process photo, analysis screenshot with student ID, calculated mass properties (weight, volume, surface area), and the finalized color scan image.

## Methodology & Execution Steps
1. **Calibration & Setup**: Align the scanner targets and mount the model on the rotary table.
2. **Texture Scanning**: Execute a 36-step color scan, ensuring HDR prevents optical clipping on the complex geometry.
3. **Mesh Processing**: Evaluate the generated STL using SolidWorks or Meshmixer to extract geometric properties.

## Toolchain & Software Used
- EinScan SE-SP V2 (Hardware & Software Suite)
- SolidWorks / Meshmixer

## Acquired Engineering Competencies
- Rotary table calibration for precision scanning
- HDR and Texture Scan optimization
- Reverse engineering of complex, textured geometries
