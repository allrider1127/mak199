# Lab 06: Optical Scanning - Human Head

## Project Objective
The mission objective is to perform a high-fidelity color scan of a Human Head model using advanced rotary table configurations, specifically optimizing for complex topological curves and surface textures.

## Engineering Constraints & Criteria
- **Hardware Calibration**: The rotary table must be utilized for 36 distinct captures (10° increments).
- **Scanning Parameters**: HDR and Texture Scan features must be activated.
- **Validation Report**: Requires a unified PDF with a scanning photograph, analysis screenshot with student ID, structural properties (weight, volume, surface area), and the color scan output.

## Methodology & Execution Steps
1. **Optical Optimization**: Configure HDR modes to accurately capture the intricate curves of the human head model.
2. **Data Acquisition**: Conduct the 36-step automated scanning process.
3. **Data Synthesis**: Export the STL and utilize analytical software to compute volume, surface area, and weight.

## Toolchain & Software Used
- EinScan SE-SP V2
- SolidWorks / Meshmixer

## Acquired Engineering Competencies
- Advanced optical hardware alignment
- HDR parameter tuning for organic shapes
- Data extraction from digital twins
