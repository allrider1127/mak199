# Lab 07: Slicing Parametric Optimization

## Project Objective
The mission objective is to evaluate the influence of slicing parameters—such as layer height, base print speed, fill pattern, and fill density—on print time and structural integrity.

## Engineering Constraints & Criteria
- **Report Structure**: A precise 3-page validation report is required.
- **Page 1**: Screenshot of the sliced model with print time visible, alongside the student ID.
- **Page 2**: Analytical explanation of the chosen layer height and print speed, detailing their impact on print time.
- **Page 3**: Visual comparison and evaluation of at least 4 different internal fill patterns (e.g., Line, Grid, Triangle).

## Methodology & Execution Steps
1. **Parameter Modification**: Adjust basic slicing variables (layer height, speed) to observe temporal changes.
2. **Infill Topology Evaluation**: Generate and analyze multiple fill patterns for structural efficiency.
3. **Data Synthesis**: Compile the findings into the standardized 3-page documentation.

## Toolchain & Software Used
- FlashPrint (or equivalent slicing engine)

## Acquired Engineering Competencies
- Additive manufacturing parameter optimization
- Internal lattice (infill) topology evaluation
- Print cycle time management
