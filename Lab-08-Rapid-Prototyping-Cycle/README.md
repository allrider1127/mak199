# Lab 08: Rapid Prototyping Cycle Validation

## Project Objective
The mission objective is to physically manufacture a previously measured component under severe temporal constraints, optimizing slicing parameters to achieve rapid iteration without hardware failure.

## Engineering Constraints & Criteria
- **Temporal Limit**: Total physical production time must strictly not exceed 15 minutes.
- **Hardware Limitations**: Optimization must utilize a standard 0.4 mm nozzle.
- **Validation**: A single-page PDF containing a photograph of the printed component, the 3D printer, and the student ID card to prove physical manufacturing.

## Methodology & Execution Steps
1. **Slicing Optimization**: Aggressively modify layer heights, infills, and print speeds in FlashPrint to bring estimated time under 15 minutes.
2. **G-Code Export**: Transfer the optimized toolpath directly to the printer.
3. **Physical Execution**: Monitor the high-speed print run and document the successful completion.

## Toolchain & Software Used
- FlashPrint
- FlashForge 3D Printer (0.4 mm nozzle)

## Acquired Engineering Competencies
- High-speed additive manufacturing
- G-Code generation and parameter tuning
- Rapid prototyping constraint management
