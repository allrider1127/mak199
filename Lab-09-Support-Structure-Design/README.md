# Lab 09: Sacrificial Support Structure Design

## Project Objective
The mission objective is to conduct comprehensive research into the design, application, and removal of support structures in additive manufacturing, applying these concepts to the complex `Bugbear.STL` geometry.

## Engineering Constraints & Criteria
- **Documentation**: A structured 5-page presentation is required, with one specific topic per page.
- **Topics Covered**: 
  1. Parameters dictating support generation (e.g., overhang angles) and support types (Tree vs. Wall).
  2. Support removal methodologies (mechanical vs. soluble).
  3. Characteristics of water-soluble support materials (e.g., PVA).
  4. Limitations of supportless 5-axis manufacturing.
  5. Application of 2 distinct support strategies on the `Bugbear.STL` model.

## Methodology & Execution Steps
1. **Theoretical Research**: Investigate the physics of overhangs and sacrificial geometries.
2. **Software Simulation**: Load the `Bugbear.STL` file into slicing software and generate two varied support structures.
3. **Report Generation**: Compile theoretical findings and simulation screenshots into the final presentation.

## Toolchain & Software Used
- FlashPrint / Ultimaker Cura / Meshmixer
- Presentation Software

## Acquired Engineering Competencies
- Overhang vector and support analysis
- Sacrificial interface geometry optimization
- Advanced additive manufacturing strategy comparison
