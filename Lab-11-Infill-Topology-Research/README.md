# Lab 11: Internal Lattice Optimization & Lightweighting

## Project Objective
The mission objective is to produce a comprehensive research paper on structural infill optimization, detailing the mechanical, design, and economic impacts of internal lattice choices in 3D printing.

## Engineering Constraints & Criteria
- **Documentation**: A 5-page PDF presentation detailing individual research.
- **Core Topics**:
  1. Definition and importance of infill structures.
  2. Types of infill patterns (e.g., grid, gyroid) and their respective advantages.
  3. The impact of infill density and thickness on print quality and strength.
  4. Design optimization techniques utilizing infill variables.
  5. The relationship between material selection, economic cost, and infill structures.

## Methodology & Execution Steps
1. **Literature Review**: Research standard additive manufacturing infill strategies.
2. **Mechanical Evaluation**: Analyze how structural strength varies with different internal topologies and densities.
3. **Synthesis**: Compile the engineering research into a structured, original 5-page presentation.

- Internal lattice optimization and lightweighting theory
- Additive manufacturing economic optimization
- Volumetric density and structural mechanics analysis
